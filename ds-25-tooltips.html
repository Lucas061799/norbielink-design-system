Tooltips

arrow_back arrow_forward

Tooltips display informative text when users hover or focus on an element. Two variants — plain and rich — serve different information needs. Note: Norbielink currently ships no custom tooltip — it uses the native title attribute throughout. These are drawn in the product’s surface language so they fit if a styled tooltip is introduced; until then, prefer title.
