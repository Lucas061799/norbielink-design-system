Elevation

arrow_back arrow_forward

Norbielink is a mostly flat interface. Elevation comes from three places: Tailwind's shadow scale applied by class, a short list of bespoke shadows for specific floating surfaces, and a brand-tinted glow reserved for primary actions. The DS previously published an invented four-step ramp (.08 / .12 / .18 / .24) that appears nowhere in the product.

Scale

Applied by class. Usage counts in the shipped bundle: shadow-2xl ×37, shadow-lg ×36, shadow-xl ×32, shadow-sm ×7, shadow-md ×1.

Brand glow

Reserved for primary actions. It is conditional: a toolbar primary sits flat, a confirming primary in a dialog or wizard glows, and a disabled primary always drops to none.
