Space

arrow_back arrow_forward

Norbielink has no 4- or 8-point grid. Spacing is a short set of values chosen per component — gaps cluster tightly around 12px, and padding is almost always an asymmetric vertical/horizontal pair tuned to the control.

Gaps

Five values cover every flex gap in the app. Counts are occurrences on a representative screen.

SampleGapSeenUsed for
4px×8Segmented-control segments, tight icon rows
6px×16Icon ↔ label inside a chip or status pill
8px×3Button content, small clusters
12px×26The default — toast parts, alert parts, form rows
16px×12Card and section groupings

Padding

Paired values, smallest to largest. The vertical figure is almost always well under the horizontal one — that ratio is what makes the product’s controls read compact.
