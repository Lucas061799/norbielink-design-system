App Bars

arrow_back arrow_forward

There are two bars, and they are not the same thing. A global app bar sits above every screen at 52px and holds search and partner branding — never a page title. Each screen then draws its own page header at 71px that carries the title and bleeds out past the content padding.

App bar

Spans the full width to the right of the sidebar. Search sits at a fixed 400px on the left, a flexible spacer fills the middle, and the partner lockup is pinned right.

Powered by

Page header

The title bar belongs to the screen, not the chrome. It uses negative margins to reach past the page’s 48px side padding and sit flush against the content area’s edges, so its rule runs the full width while the title stays on the 28px inset.

The app bar never holds the page title, a back button or page actions. It carries search and the partner lockup only, and is identical on every screen.
The title is font-weight 400 at 22px. Everything else large in the product is 600 or 700, so this is deliberate and easy to get wrong.
The search field’s values are a 0.78125 scaling of a 1 / 14 / 10 / 16 / 44 / 46 set — an export artifact that shipped. They are recorded as they render; nothing else in the product carries sub-pixel values.
Navigation lives in the sidebar, not up here. The two never share controls.
