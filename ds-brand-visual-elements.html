Along with our logo, these visual cues were created to emphasize our brand on printed and digital materials.

Usage

These tiles act as the visual representation of our product suite. They serve as intuitive entry points, designed to help users instantly recognize and navigate to specific lines of business.

Usage

These items aren’t used everywhere. They’re primarily used to tie the brand together. Examples would be leaves on an initial page or entry point to a branding piece, like letterhead, envelopes, business cards, the home page of the website, or the cover of a quarterly report.

Monstera Leaves

Monstera leaves. Named from the Latin word for “monstrous” or “abnormal”, referring to the unusually large leaves, they are native to Norbie’s environment.
