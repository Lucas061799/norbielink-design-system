Lists

arrow_back arrow_forward

The product’s only list is the activity timeline on a client record. Everything else that looks like a list is a table. A filter row sits above it; the entries themselves live on a white card with a connector line running down the icon rail.

Activity timeline

Each entry type has its own icon colour on a 12% tint of the same hue. The six types are fixed. The vertical connector is drawn between discs and stops at the last entry.

These six are decorative type markers, not semantic colours. #F59E0B here means “email”, not “warning”, and #F97316 means “note”, not “danger”. Do not reuse them for state.
For tabular data use a table. This timeline exists because activity entries are heterogeneous and have no columns.
When the filter matches nothing the card shows a centred empty state: a 32×32 muted icon over No activity found at 13px.
