Snackbar

arrow_back arrow_forward

Snackbars confirm an action just after it happens. Norbielink uses a white card toast anchored to the top-right, carrying a title and a short description — used for “Profile saved”, “Email sent” and the auth flow. It has two kinds, default and warning, and never carries an action.

Variants
check
Profile saved
Your user name has been updated.
mail
Email sent
We’ve sent a password reset link to your inbox. Open it to set a new password.

Can’t save draft yet
Fill in Agency Name, Code, Contact, and Email first.
