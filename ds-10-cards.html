Cards

arrow_back arrow_forward

Three card types ship in the product, all at 16px radius on a white surface with a 1px #E5E7EB hairline and no shadow. The DS previously published a “marketplace card” with teal icon tiles, category dots, N MARKETS counters and a gradient top bar, and a “ProSuite feature card” — none of which exist in Norbielink.

LOB tile

The marketplace product picker. A 60px round grey disc holding the product’s own 38px PNG icon — the same asset the app serves from /insurance-icons/, with a centred two-line label beneath.
