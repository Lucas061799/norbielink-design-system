Typography

arrow_back arrow_forward

Montserrat throughout. The type system is small and flat: eleven sizes, four weights, and a line-height that is 1.5× the font size almost everywhere. Letter-spacing is normal except in three places.

Scale

Measured off the running app. Line-height is inherited rather than declared, which is why it tracks 1.5× so consistently — a rule that sets font-size and forgets line-height will render ~1.2× and read noticeably tight.

Weights

Four, and no more. 800 and 900 do not appear anywhere in the product — the DS used to carry 800 in 34 places.

SampleWeightUsed for
Montserrat400Body, table cells, tab labels
Montserrat500Nav items, secondary labels, status chips
Montserrat600Buttons, badges, card titles, emphasis
Montserrat700KPI numbers, dialog titles — used sparingly

Rules

Set line-height whenever you set font-size. Omitting it falls back to normal (~1.2×), which is visibly tighter than the product.
Use 1.5 unless you are matching one of the three exceptions: the 24px KPI (leading-none), the dialog body (1.625), or badge labels (a flat 16px).
Leave tracking at normal. The only deliberate exceptions are the 24px KPI (-0.6px), uppercase 10px labels (1px) and the NEW badge (0.9px).
Never reach past 700.
