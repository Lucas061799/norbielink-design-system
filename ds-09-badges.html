Badges

arrow_back arrow_forward

Badges communicate membership tiers, system capabilities, appointment state, and record status. Norbielink has two families: pill badges at 9999px, and status chips at 6px whose chip is always neutral and whose 6px dot carries the only colour. Everything on this page is transcribed from the live app.

Two families

Norbielink has two badge families and they are not interchangeable. Pill badges (9999px) mark an attribute of a thing — a tier, a flag, an appointment. Status chips (6px) report the state of a record in a table, and they are always neutral: only the dot carries colour.

Tonal pill badges

Two tonal states, seen on the Agencies list. Brand carries a 5% gradient wash with gradient text; teal is a flat 10% tint. There is no success / warning / error trio — Norbielink does not have one.

Status chips

Every status renders the same neutral chip. Only the 6px dot changes, and there are exactly three dot colours. Adding a status means picking one of the three — it never means adding a colour.

Status pill (tonal)

The second status component. Where the chip above keeps the surface neutral and colours only the dot, this one tints the whole pill. Both ship in the product — the tonal pill on the Agencies and Clients lists, the neutral chip in the Quotes table. Pick by surface, not by taste.

Indicator icons

An 18px round mark that trails a cell value — in the Agencies table it follows the Last Login date. It is not a badge: there is no label and no padding, and the meaning is carried entirely by the title tooltip, so it is only ever used where the row already gives context.

Don't
Give a status its own fill colour. The chip is neutral; the dot is the signal.
Add a fourth dot colour — map the status onto done / active / idle.
Use red, amber or green. None of them exist in Norbielink.
Make a status chip a pill, or a pill badge 6px.
Use .badge-new's solid gradient for anything but NEW and the active filter.
