Date Picker

arrow_back arrow_forward

A read-only text field with a calendar icon that opens the calendar panel beneath it. One implementation ships, used for Appt. Date and the two Expiration Date fields on an agency record. The value is always MM/DD/YYYY.

Closed and open

The field inherits whatever input style surrounds it — on an agency record that is the 8px-radius form field. Only the icon and the readonly behaviour belong to the picker.

Date Picker and Calendar are one component, not two. This page documents the trigger; the Calendar page documents the panel and its three views. Neither ships without the other.
Clicking outside closes the panel and resets it to the day view, so it never reopens on the month or year grid.
There is no clear button, no “today” shortcut and no keyboard entry. A date is changed only by opening the panel and picking one.
Fields elsewhere in the product use a 10px radius; the record editor this control lives in uses 8px. The picker follows its surroundings rather than carrying its own.
