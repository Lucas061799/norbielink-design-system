Skeleton

arrow_back arrow_forward

A stack of flat grey bars standing in for text that has not arrived. The product uses a single bar height and varies only the width — there is no shimmer, no pulse and no rounded avatar placeholder.

Text skeleton

Transcribed from the document preview pane: fourteen bars, each 8px tall, widths cycling between roughly 65% and 95%.
