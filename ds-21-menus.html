Menus

arrow_back arrow_forward

Menus display a list of choices on a temporary surface. They appear when users interact with a button, icon, or control and dismiss when an item is selected or the user clicks away.

Overview

Menus use a white card surface at 12px radius with a #E5E7EB hairline and a 180px minimum width. Hover is neutral — #F9FAFB (rgba(255,255,255,0.04) in dark), not a brand tint. Only the active row and destructive items carry colour.

States

Three states, not four. There is no disabled menu item anywhere in the product, and hover is never a purple fill — it is a neutral #F9FAFB tint in all three menus. Selection is marked with a trailing check, not with weight.

Select menu — the common case
rest transparent · #1F2937
hover #F9FAFB · colour unchanged
selected rgba(168,85,247,.08) · #A614C3 + check
Account menu — icons and a section label
My Account
rest transparent · #2D3653
hover #F9FAFB · colour unchanged
Items keep a 12px radius, unlike the other two.
Context menu — right-click on a row
rest #1F2937 · hover #F9FAFB
Destructive items are #EF4444 in every state.
No separator, no disabled row.

Three patterns ship. They do not share a radius, a shadow or an item shape — pick the one that matches the trigger.
