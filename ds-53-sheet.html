Sheet

arrow_back arrow_forward

A sheet enters from the right edge, full height, with a 1px left border and a shadow-2xl. Nothing enters from the bottom, left or top. A sheet’s width is a viewport fraction — 50vw, 58vw or 70vw — which is what separates it from a drawer, the fixed 480px panel. Two forms ship: a viewer that opens over the page, and an expand-in-place panel that is an inline card until you expand it.

Viewer sheet

Opens a single record over the page — a note, a document, a statement. The header is a breadcrumb plus icon actions; the scrim is a click-to-close strip on the sheet’s left.

The two forms scrim differently. A viewer puts the scrim inside its own fixed box as a flex-1 sibling, so only the strip beside the sheet is dimmed and clickable. An expand-in-place panel uses a separate fixed inset-0 layer at z-40, dimming the whole page.
A sheet’s width is always vw; the one pixel value here is a min-width. A panel with a fixed width is a drawer — different element, different shadow, different header.
Expand-in-place is not a resize: it is two fixed layouts. See Resizable.
Nothing enters from the bottom, left or top — checked across the whole app. A centred overlay is a dialog, not a sheet.
