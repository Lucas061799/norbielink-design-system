Tabs

arrow_back arrow_forward

Two tab patterns ship. The underline bar switches the main view of a record; the pill group switches a sub-view inside a panel. Neither uses a border on the item itself — the active underline is a separate 2px gradient element.

Underline bar

The record tab bar. The container carries a 1px hairline and the active tab paints a 2px brand gradient over it.

Pill group

Used for sub-views. The selected pill takes a neutral #F3F4F6 fill — no brand colour, no underline.
