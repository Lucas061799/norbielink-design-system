Calendar

arrow_back arrow_forward

The calendar is not a standalone component. It is the popover half of the date picker — a 280px panel that opens under a read-only input. One implementation ships, and its title button cycles the panel through three views: day → month → year.

The three views

Clicking the title moves out one level; picking a value moves back in. The header arrows step by one month, one year, or twelve years depending on the view.

Cell states

One function styles every cell in all three views, so a selected month looks exactly like a selected day.

StateFillTextWeightBorder
RestTransparent#1F29375001px transparent

TodayTransparent#A855F77001px rgba(168,85,247,.45)
SelectedBrand gradient#fff7001px transparent
Today is #A855F7, not #A614C3. That value is hard-coded and does not change between light and dark mode — it is the only place in the product where the dark-mode purple appears on a light surface. Treat it as the today marker’s own colour, not as the brand accent.
Today and selected are mutually exclusive: once a date is selected the today ring is suppressed on that cell, so a cell never carries both.
There is no range selection, no multi-date selection, no disabled days, no min/max and no footer. A click commits the value and closes the panel.
Leading blanks are empty <div>s, not greyed-out days from the previous month.
