Dialogs

arrow_back arrow_forward

A dialog is a 16px panel on a rgba(0,0,0,.45) scrim, sized to its job rather than to a scale: 400px for a confirmation, 540–620px for a working panel, capped at 92vw and 86vh. Header and footer are fixed; only the body scrolls.

Panel dialog

Title with a supporting line, a close button, a scrolling body, and a footer whose buttons sit at opposite ends.
