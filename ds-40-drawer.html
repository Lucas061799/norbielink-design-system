Drawer

arrow_back arrow_forward

A fixed panel on the right edge, full height. It is the only surface in the product with a horizontal shadow. Drawer and Sheet are the same family — this page covers the simple titled panel, Sheet covers the record viewer and the expand-in-place variant, and both share the position, edge and shadow below.
