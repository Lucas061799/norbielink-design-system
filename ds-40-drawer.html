Drawer

arrow_back arrow_forward

A right-edge panel at a fixed 480px, capped at 90vw. That fixed width is what separates it from a sheet, which is always a viewport fraction — and the two do not share a shadow or a header either. This is the only surface in the product with a horizontal shadow.

Drawer

Used to read one piece of correspondence. The header carries an eyebrow, the reference and date, a PRINT action and a close button; the body is the only scrolling region.

Page content stays in place behind the drawer.

Drawer vs sheet: a drawer is 480px fixed with a horizontal shadow and an eyebrow header; a sheet is 50/58/70vw with shadow-2xl and either a breadcrumb or an inline card header. Both pin to the right edge, so width is the reliable tell.
Its scrim is a separate full-screen layer at 0.35, the same as an expand-in-place sheet — not the 0.25 strip a record viewer uses.
The same content is reachable inline as an accordion row. The drawer is the “open it properly” route, which is why it carries PRINT and the accordion does too.
